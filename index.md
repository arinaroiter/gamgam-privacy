

# Gam Gam – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 11 October 2026*

Gam Gam is a free backgammon game for Android developed by Arina Roiter ("we").

## Data we collect

Gam Gam has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number,
location, contacts, or any other personal information.

When you play an **online game**, the app sends the following to our game server:

- The room code you create or join
- Your dice rolls and moves
- Any chat messages you type during the game

This data is used only to run the game between you and your opponent. It is not
linked to your identity, is not used for advertising or analytics, and is not
shared with or sold to any third party. Room data and chat messages are discarded
when the game ends.

Local games (Classic / pass-and-play) do not send any data anywhere.

## Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## Children

Gam Gam does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children
under 13.
